# When You Open the Qur'an — treatment

Owais's 11-scene script (`script.md`), about 12 minutes, for Muslim youth. Humble, warm, no faces. Title changed by Owais to "When You Open the Qur'an".

## Three candidate structures
1. **Portfolio** (a style per scene, as in Deebacha). Most variety; reads as a style reel over 12 minutes and every switch costs attention.
2. **Two worlds** (the reader's desk vs the world the Qur'an spoke into), joined by one light. Unified; matches the film's claim that the same page deepens as you understand it.
3. **Two worlds plus two** (scene 3 as projection on a white city model, scene 9 as silkscreen).

**Choice: 2** (council, unanimous; `council-verdict.md`), with 3 offered to Owais as samples.

## The two worlds
- **Desk ("you") — Impasto.** Knife-painted room, morning light raking across the ridges, one warm colour event on the mushaf. The reader is only hands, sleeve, shoulder, back.
- **Screen ("them", history) — Shadow screen.** Carved translucent hide on a cotton screen lit by oil lamps; girih and arabesque cutwork; figures still, from behind, sliding only. No prophet shown.
- One light carries across: window sun = puppet lamp = stars.

## Rules
- Nothing is drawn on screen. Reveals: light rising/moving, cut pieces sliding or lifting in, sheets laid down, paint-over sweeps of finished planes.
- Arabic only as verified mushaf type, crisp, never painted.
- No music; sparse foley (paper turn, lamp, room tone).
